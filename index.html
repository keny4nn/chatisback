The stream’s Twitch chat, back on its YouTube™ replay.

ChatIsBack finds the stream on its own and plays its chat next to the video, synced to the moment each message was written, even on an edited replay. When it isn’t sure, the label at the top says so.

Add to Chrome Free, no account, for Chrome on a computer.
